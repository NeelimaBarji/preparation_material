## Research workflow

1. Define the research question and intended contribution.
2. Search and record relevant literature with source details.
3. Document inclusion/exclusion criteria and methodology.
4. Track assumptions, evidence quality, limitations, and open questions.
5. Keep protocol versions and analysis decisions reproducible.

Research documents are working notes, not clinical guidance. Verify all medical or scientific claims against authoritative sources before use.
